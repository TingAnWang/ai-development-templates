# Changelog

## Unreleased — practical workflow companion (prepared November 10, 2026)

Readers of the new article series can now find dedicated task, receipt, handoff,
claim-map, and acceptance materials beside the continuing role specifications.

### Added

- Six compact templates: outcome task, delivery receipt, optional preparation/
  execution controls, handoff, claim/evidence map, and requirement/test matrix.
- Three worked examples with filled companion documents: viewer completion,
  coordination through a publication hold, and annotation-history validation.
- A [series map](series/practical_ai_workflows.md) connecting article titles,
  starting templates, examples, and the earlier public baseline.

### Updated

- README navigation, first-task guidance, and description of what the collection
  includes. The former statement that dedicated task/receipt templates were
  absent now points to the supplied files.

### Preserved and limited

- All seven existing files under `00_shared` through `04_pure_reviewer` and
  their paths are unchanged.
- The [October 17 baseline](https://github.com/TingAnWang/ai-development-templates/tree/52a4c5dab91d7220cb3afc5dfd18b8bd0b5cf51d)
  remains the reference for previously published wording.
- Example results are historical reports with explicit limits; proposed
  annotation implementation checks are not presented as executed.
- No application, validator, private dataset, or experimental reproduction is
  included. The template adaptations have not been performance-evaluated.
- Medium URLs and a named release are to be added after review/publication.
